# Role 1 — section map
**Goal:** Turn B2B visitors into qualified enquiries by making scope, process, fit, and contact routes clear.
**Flow:** Arrive → understand offer → scan services → see delivery approach → assess fit → resolve questions → enquire/call/WhatsApp.

| Order | Section | Wireframe and purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sticky header | Compact brand, anchor navigation and prominent consultation button; mobile menu collapses links. |
| 2 | Hero | Large promise and two direct actions beside a workplace image; trust values below. |
| 3 | Services | Six icon-led cards to let prospects find the relevant capability quickly. |
| 4 | Process | Dark-green feature band with six numbered steps and a sustainability image, clarifying end-to-end coverage. |
| 5 | Why us | Editorial introduction plus five succinct reasons to partner. No unverified metrics. |
| 6 | Industries | Simple chips for the supplied sectors; client confirmation is flagged in documentation. |
| 7 | Testimonials | Hidden until approved client quotes exist. |
| 8 | FAQ | Accessible disclosure rows to reduce objections without increasing page clutter. |
| 9 | Contact | Form first, contact methods and map placeholder alongside; primary conversion destination. |
| 10 | Final CTA | Last short consultation prompt for readers reaching the bottom. |
| 11 | Footer | Brand, repeated anchors, contact and copyright. |
