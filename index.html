EHS | Environment | Sustainability | ESG | EPR — Consultancy & Training

What does an EHS consultant do?+

An EHS consultant helps organisations manage workplace safety, environmental responsibility, and legal compliance. This includes risk assessments, audits, system development, and training.

Which industries need EHS compliance support?+

Any organisation with employees, operations, or environmental impact can benefit, especially manufacturing, construction, warehousing, and chemical sectors.

What is ESG and why does it matter?+

ESG stands for Environmental, Social, and Governance. It describes how responsibly a business operates, and it is increasingly important to customers, investors, and regulators.

What is EPR compliance?+

Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) requires producers, importers, and brand owners to take responsibility for managing the waste their products create. We help you understand and meet these obligations.

Do you provide safety training for employees?+

Yes. We offer training and capacity-building programmes for teams at different levels. Contact us to discuss your needs.
